# Fawad Gems — Entrepreneurship Pitch Deck & Business Model

**Handcrafted excellence with real stones** — *Designed with Purpose, Worn with Pride*[cite: 6, 10]

Fawad Gems is a Karachi-based jewelry and gemstone startup bridging ethical sourcing with bespoke craftsmanship. Combining raw natural gemstones from Northern Pakistan with Silver 925 artisan work in Saddar, the venture delivers authentic, modern, and story-driven jewelry without the markups of luxury showrooms or the tarnishing and imitation risks of mass-market street jewelry[cite: 7, 8, 9, 13, 14].

---

## Problem & Solution

* **Market Problem:** Buyers in Karachi face a sharp gap between fast-tarnishing artificial jewelry and high-end 21K gold or luxury showrooms, while street-level vendors often carry imitation stones or dated designs.
* **Our Solution:** Small-batch Silver 925 and natural gemstone pieces featuring **Raw-Fine Hybrid** designs (blending unrefined raw stones with polished facets), **The Storyteller Series** (QR-linked digital origin stories), interactive wooden packaging, and the **Nikhar Promise** (lifetime re-polishing care)[cite: 8, 9, 10, 12, 13].

---

## Product Collection & Customer Segments

* **Starter Collection (Students & Gen Z):** Everyday rings and studs crafted for modern, minimalist wear.
* **Mid-Range Collection (Working Professionals):** Signature Raw-Fine Hybrid pendants and rings designed for daily durability and distinction.
* **Signature Collection (Gift Givers & Collectors):** Bespoke, custom-designed statement pieces paired with interactive story packaging[cite: 7, 10, 12].

---

## Sourcing, Operations & Market Opportunity

* **Sourcing & Supply Chain:** Natural gemstones sourced from Northern Pakistan—including emeralds from Mingora (Swat Valley), lapis lazuli via Peshawar's Namak Mandi, tourmaline from Stak Nala and Chitral, and aquamarine and topaz from Skardu and Gilgit-Baltistan—crafted by Saddar artisans under a Just-in-Time (JIT) faceting model with insured TCS/Leopard logistics.
* **Market Opportunity (Karachi):**
  * **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** ~7,000,000 women aged 18–60 in Karachi
  * **Serviceable Available Market (SAM):** ~1,200,000 tech-savvy women shopping online or via Instagram
  * **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):** ~6,000 customers in Year 1 (0.5% of SAM)
* **Channels & Revenue Streams:** Direct-to-consumer sales, recurring re-plating and polishing services, and weekend pop-up experiences ("Stone & Sip" cafe collaborations), promoted through TikTok/Instagram Reels ("Behind-the-Stone"), WhatsApp Business catalogs, and curated local pop-ups[cite: 7, 11, 13, 14, 16, 17].

---

## Seed Allocation & Go-To-Market Roadmap

* **Capital Allocation:**
  * **40% Inventory:** Bulk sourcing of raw emeralds, lapis lazuli, and aquamarine
  * **30% Marketing:** TikTok/Instagram creator collaborations and "Behind-the-Stone" video production
  * **20% Pop-Up Tour:** 12 weekend kiosks across Karachi cafes and malls
  * **10% Certification:** Pre-certifying signature pieces through GGI labs
* **Execution Roadmap:**
  * **Months 1–2 (Foundation):** Finalize supply contracts with Swat and Skardu mine-wholesalers; begin 3D modeling and Raw-Fine Hybrid prototyping with Saddar artisans.
  * **Months 3–4 (Brand Identity):** Build interactive QR-linked wooden stone boxes; launch teaser campaigns on TikTok and Instagram.
  * **Months 5–6 (Launch & Validation):** Launch e-commerce storefront and WhatsApp Business catalog; host the first "Stone & Sip" pop-up in Karachi.
  * **Month 7+ (Optimization):** Refine designs based on customer feedback and scale marketing around top-performing stone categories[cite: 17, 18].
